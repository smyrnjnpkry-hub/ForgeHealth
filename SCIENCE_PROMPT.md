## What the research actually says

Procrastination is **voluntary delay of an intended action despite expecting to be worse off** (Steel, 2007). It is not laziness and not a time-management hole.

### 1. Temporal Motivation Theory (Steel & König, 2006; Steel, 2007)

```
Motivation = (Expectancy × Value) / (1 + Impulsiveness × Delay)
```

People stall when the finish feels far, uncertain, or unrewarding, especially if they are impulse-sensitive. Strong predictors from Steel’s 691-correlation meta-analysis: **task aversiveness, delay, low self-efficacy, impulsiveness, low conscientiousness**. Weak: neuroticism, rebelliousness, sensation seeking.

**App lever:** raise expectancy (ugly first slice), raise value (why + tiny after-reward), cut delay (2 minutes), cut impulsiveness (if-then plan).

### 2. Mood repair, not missing calendars (Tice; Sirois & Pychyl, 2013)

We delay to feel better *now*. The task is aversive; escape is the regulation strategy. Shame after delay is itself aversive, which feeds the next delay. Emotion-regulation skill training reduces later procrastination (Eckert et al., 2022). CBT for procrastination has a moderate effect (Rozental et al., 2018, *g* ≈ 0.55). Time-management advice alone is weak.

**App lever:** label the feeling (boring / unclear / too-big / fear / no-reward / tired / distracted), then move. Never shame a miss. Never zero a streak of *starts* because of one skipped day.

### 3. Implementation intentions and WOOP

If-then plans beat vague goals (Gollwitzer; meta-analysis *d* ≈ 0.65 across 94 studies; 2024 review of 642 tests: contingent if-then format, high motivation, at least one rehearsal). **WOOP** (Oettingen) = Wish, Outcome, Obstacle, Plan — mental contrasting plus implementation intentions. Fantasizing the outcome without the obstacle does not help.

**App lever:** every plan is `If [cue], then [action]`. Obstacle is required.

### 4. Tiny Habits / Fogg B = MAP

Behavior happens when Motivation, Ability, and a Prompt coincide. Shrink the action until it is doable on a bad day. Recipe: **After [anchor], I will [tiny behavior], then [celebrate].** Emotion at the celebration wires the habit faster than grim repetition. Simpler behaviors automate faster (Lally: exercise took ~1.5× as long as drinking water).

**App lever:** default “two-minute start.” If someone skips often, the habit is still too big.

### 5. Automaticity timeline (Lally et al., 2010; Singh et al., 2024)

Median time to peak automaticity: **~66 days** (range 18–254). The 21-day myth is not habit research. **Missing a single day did not flatten the curve.** Context-stable cues (same time, place, preceding action) speed encoding in the basal ganglia (Graybiel; Wendy Wood). Once cued by context, the loop needs less self-control (Stojanovic & Wood, 2024).

**App lever:** count repetitions and a 1–7 automaticity rating, not hostage streaks. Copy: “One miss is data, not a reset.”

### 6. Identity over streak theatre

“I am someone who starts” stays available when mood is low. Streaks are a report. Shields / pause for illness or travel beat punishing a flu week with a red zero.

## Product to build (Forge tab, replacing Watch)

A local-first **Start workshop** on `/forge`. Core loop must work at 1 a.m. offline.

1. **Unstick (primary).** Name the task → tag the aversion → write a 2-minute first action + a reward you get the moment the two minutes end → 120s timer (skip allowed) → log started / finished slice / still stuck. Starting earns XP. Shame copy is banned.
2. **Tiny recipes.** After [anchor] I will [tiny] then [celebrate]. One tap to mark done today. Cap celebration as a felt spark, not a slot machine.
3. **WOOP cards.** Wish, Outcome, Obstacle, If-then. Delete allowed. Rehearse by reading the if-then aloud (optional voice).
4. **Style quiz (once).** Mood-avoider / Perfectionist / Now-bias / Overloaded. Result is a default move, not a diagnosis.
5. **Lab.** Eight science cards with the citation and the in-app use. Automaticity estimate vs 66-day median.
6. **Home.** Giant “Stuck? Unstick in 2 minutes.” Today’s recipes. Identity line.
7. **Habits.** Show the cue. Automaticity bar from unique days done. Two-minute start is a default habit. Copy that a miss does not erase the groove.
8. **You.** Editable identity sentence. Style result. Local-only data note.
